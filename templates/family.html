Family Nutrition Tips
  • 🥘 One-pot meals like dal, khichdi, and sabzi can be customised for each member with minor tweaks.
  • 🧂 Low-salt cooking benefits everyone; use amchur, lime, and herbs for flavour.
  • 🌾 Switch to millets — jowar rotis benefit all ages from kids to seniors.
  • 🥛 Dairy — curd and milk daily for calcium across all age groups.
  • 🍫 Healthy desserts — date-nut ladoos, ragi halwa instead of maida sweets.
  • 🚰 Keep water accessible for children; add sabza seeds or cucumber slices for variety.
